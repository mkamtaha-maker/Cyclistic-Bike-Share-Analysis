# 🚴 Case Study: Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

## 1. Introduction
* **Purpose:** Analyze how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently to help design data-driven marketing strategies for converting casual riders into annual members.
* **Real-World Context:** Maximize recurring subscription revenue and long-term customer lifetime value for a major urban bike-share system in Chicago.

---

## 2. Problems & Analysis
Through data cleaning and aggregation in **Google BigQuery (SQL)** and visualization in **Tableau**, several critical differences were identified:

* **Ride Volume:** Annual members account for ~74% of all trips (~1.19M rides), forming the operational backbone.
* **Ride Duration:** Casual riders ride more than **2x longer** per trip (~27.3 mins) compared to members (~11.7 mins).
* **Weekly Trends:** Members peak during weekdays (commuting to work), while casual usage surges on weekends (recreational riding).

---

## 3. Proposed Solutions & Alternatives
To convert casual riders, we evaluated two potential strategies:

* **Option A: Blanket Discounts on Annual Passes (Alternative)**
  * *Pros:* Easy to implement across all user segments.
  * *Cons:* Low conversion efficiency; dilutes brand value for commuters who would pay full price.
* **Option B: Targeted Weekend & Duration-Based Marketing (Recommended Solution)**
  * *Pros:* Directly targets high-duration casual riders during peak weekend hours; high conversion precision.
  * *Cons:* Requires coordinated digital marketing efforts during specific timeframe windows.

---

## 4. Conclusion
The analysis proves that casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic bikes for entirely different purposes (recreation vs. utility commuting). Strategic marketing must address these distinct behavioral motivators rather than treating all users the same.

---

## 5. Next Steps & Strategic Recommendations
1. **Weekend Digital Campaigns:** Launch targeted in-app promotions and station signage on Friday afternoons through Sunday (Executed by Marketing Team / Q3 2026).
2. **Value-Based Messaging:** Highlight cost savings for casual riders whose average trips exceed 20 minutes (Executed by Content Team / Q3 2026).
3. **Seasonal Commuter Passes:** Introduce 3-month summer trial passes to build daily habits before converting to full annual plans (Executed by Operations & Marketing / Q2-Q3 2026).
